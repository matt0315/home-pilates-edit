About The Home Pilates Edit

This is a small Australian site with original notes on pilates and home-exercise products you can buy here: grip socks, clothing, mats, props and reformers.

The aim is practical. We look at the current price, what is actually in the box, shipping and return catches, and who an item seems to suit. We would rather say “we have not used this ourselves” than write a pretend studio review.

We are not a pilates studio, not a physiotherapist, and not the seller of these products. Nothing here is medical advice. If you are returning from injury, talk to a qualified practitioner before you buy a machine or change the way you train.

Some photographs on the site are AI-generated illustrations, and they are labelled as such. They are not photos of us wearing the exact product, and they are not customer reviews. Where an illustration may differ from the real item, we say so on the page.

We are applying to Australian affiliate programs so that, if you buy through a link, the site can earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Until those applications are approved, the buttons go to ordinary product pages with no tracking tag. There is a full explanation on the Affiliate & AI disclosure page.

Prices are checked in Australia/Perth and dated on each note. Retailers change them, often during sales, so always confirm the figure on the product page before you order.
