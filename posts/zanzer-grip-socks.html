If a reformer class asks you to take your shoes off, you need some kind of grip on your feet. A four-pack of generic studio socks is the cheapest way to get there. ZANZER’s pilates grip socks are, on the day we checked, sitting at number one on Amazon AU’s Women’s Yoga Socks bestsellers list, with a 4.5-star average from 180 ratings.

The listing we opened (ASIN B0D3DT4YXK) was $14.44 for a four-pack, down from a typical $16.99. That is a few dollars a pair, which is the point. The stated mix is about 90% cotton with a little nylon and spandex, and a silicone grip on the sole. There are a lot of colour and pack options on the same page, and the category list showed some of those options even cheaper, so the figure that matters is the one next to the pack you actually add to the cart.

Who it suits

This is a starter or spare-pair buy: you want something for a first class, a home mat, or a spare in the gym bag, and you do not want to spend $20-plus on a single branded pair yet. It also suits anyone who is happy with a one-size, no-frills sock and a modest review count, rather than a sized Australian brand with a long studio reputation.

Pros

  • Low price per pair when you buy a multi-pack, with Prime-style delivery on an Amazon-fulfilled listing.
  • Cotton-heavy fabric and a full-ish silicone grip, according to the listing, which is what you actually need on a carriage or a hard floor.
  • Plenty of colour combinations if you want a spare set without paying for a fashion print.

Cons

  • One-size listings are a compromise. If you have a small or a large foot, a sized sock from a specialist brand is usually the safer fit.
  • Review volume is modest compared with long-running studio brands, and Amazon ratings are not the same thing as a wear-test.
  • We have not worn these. The illustration on this page is AI-generated and is not a photograph of this exact pair.

If you already go to reformer classes a few times a week, it is worth comparing this pack with a sized Australian pair such as MoveActive or All Fenix, which cost more each but should last as a proper studio staple. If you just need to get through the door without slipping, a $14 four-pack is a reasonable first step.
