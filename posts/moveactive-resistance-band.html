MoveActive’s medium loop band, for legs and side-lying work

$28 AUD

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) for the Medium Resistance Band in black: $28 AUD. Listed dimensions are 32 cm × 8 cm. Free shipping over $65.

This is a short loop band, not a long therapy roll you stand on for mat work. MoveActive’s medium band in black is $28, listed at 32 cm by 8 cm, with a polyester outer, a latex inner, and a small travel bag. The brand recommends it for pilates, yoga, barre and HIIT.

That size and construction matter, because “resistance band” covers three different tools. A long flat band is what a lot of classical mat work is written for. A loop like this is what you put around your thighs for side-lying and standing work, or above the knees on a reformer. If you were hoping for a Theraband-style roll, this is not that product. On the official page it had 68 reviews and an average a little under 4.9 when we looked, including a useful note that the medium grade suits someone who is no longer a beginner and not yet very strong.

Who it suits

Home and studio users who want a fabric-covered loop that stays put, in a mid resistance, and who are happy paying a specialist-brand price for one band. It is not the buy for a first, cheap set of three latex loops, and it is not a long band for roll-downs and footwork.

Pros

  • Fabric outer should roll up less than a bare latex loop, which is the usual complaint in glute and side-lying work.
  • Published size, a travel bag, and a clear “medium” grade, plus an Australian site with free shipping over $65.
  • A decent review count for a single prop, with people mentioning that it stays in place.

Cons

  • It contains latex in the inner layer. If you have a latex allergy, this is not a safe assumption even if one reviewer with a mild sensitivity said they were fine. Choose a labelled latex-free band instead.
  • $28 is a lot for one loop when a three-pack of basic bands can cost less than this on Amazon. You are paying for the cover, the brand and the hope it lasts.
  • We have not used this band. The illustration is original artwork, not a product photograph.

Pair it with the MoveActive ring if you want two props that will actually get used, and you can clear the free-shipping minimum in one order. If you need a long flat band for classical mat work, look for that format specifically — including a Theraband-style roll — rather than forcing this loop to do a job it was not cut for.
