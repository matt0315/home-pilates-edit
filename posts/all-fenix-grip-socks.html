All Fenix pilates grip socks: the $17 Australian pair

$17 AUD$24.99

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) for the black pair. The page showed $17, marked down from $24.99, during All Fenix’s Black Friday sale period (29 October to 30 November 2026). All Fenix is an Australian activewear label, and its pilates grip socks are a simple, one-size pair sold from the brand’s own site. On the day we checked, the black pair was $17, marked down from $24.99, as part of a Black Friday sale running from 29 October to 30 November 2026.

The listing describes a cotton-blend sock (80% cotton, 15% polyester, 5% spandex) with a rib texture, a silicone grip sole, and the brand’s mark embroidered or printed in the grip. It is sold as one size, one pair. That is a very different proposition from a four-pack of generic Amazon socks: you are paying for a local brand, a neater finish, and a known AU returns process — except during this sale.

Who it suits

Someone who wants a straightforward black studio sock from an Australian label, and who is happy with a one-size fit. It also suits a gift or a first “proper” pair if you have been making do with supermarket socks. It is less ideal if you need a precise size, or if you might want to send them back during the sale period.

Pros

  • Australian brand and a clear, simple product: one pair, black, cotton blend, silicone sole.
  • $17 is a noticeable drop from $24.99, which brings it closer to the mid-range rather than a splurge.
  • Standard shipping inside Australia is listed, with free shipping once you spend $150, which is realistic if you are adding a pair of tights.

Cons

  • Sale pairs in this Black Friday window are listed as final sale, unless they arrive faulty. Do not buy them as a “I’ll just exchange the size” experiment.
  • One size only. If you are between sizes on other brands, that is a real risk.
  • On a $17 sale price, a $11.15 shipping charge for a single pair is steep. It makes more sense as part of a larger order, or after the sale if you can wait for a regular-price return window.
  • We have not worn this pair, and the illustration is original artwork, not a brand photo.

Compared with MoveActive’s $22.95 crew sock, All Fenix is cheaper right now but offers less sizing detail and a stricter sale-return rule. Compared with a ZANZER multi-pack, you get one better-finished pair rather than several budget ones. If the sale is still on and you already have an All Fenix order meeting the free-shipping threshold, this is a tidy add-on. If you need to return a missized sock, wait until the final-sale window closes.
