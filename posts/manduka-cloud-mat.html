Manduka’s 12 mm Cloud mat for floor pilates at home

$229.95 AUD

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) on manduka.com.au: $229.95 AUD for the CLOUD Pilates Mat 12 mm. The site advertised free shipping on orders from $200.

Floor pilates is harder on the spine, hips and knees than it looks, especially if you are on timber or a thin travel mat. Manduka’s CLOUD pilates mat is a 12 mm, closed-cell mat made for that kind of work at home. On manduka.com.au, on the day we checked, it was $229.95, with free shipping from $200, so a single mat qualifies.

The listed size is 173 by 61 cm, at 2 kg, in a natural rubber and EVA foam blend, made in Taiwan. Manduka describes a dense, stable feel rather than a squashy gym mat, a surface that wipes clean, and an embossed pattern. There is a brand mark in the design; our illustration does not reproduce it. A new rubber mat can smell at first — the care notes tell you to air it out — and it should be wiped, not soaked, and kept out of the sun.

Who it suits

People who do mat work at home several times a week and want real cushioning without buying a reformer. It also suits anyone coming back from cranky wrists or hips who finds a 4 or 5 mm yoga mat unforgiving. It is not the right product if you need a cover for a reformer carriage (that is the MoveActive mat), and it is a lot of money if you only practise occasionally and already have a decent yoga mat.

Pros

  • 12 mm is a genuine pilates thickness, with a stated dense construction rather than a soft, unstable foam.
  • Closed-cell surface that wipes clean, which is more practical for daily home use than a porous open-cell mat.
  • Free shipping on this item alone from the Australian site, at a confirmed $229.95.

Cons

  • $229.95 is a premium mat price. A perfectly usable mid-range yoga mat costs less, if you do not need this much cushion.
  • At 173 by 61 cm it is a standard length, not an extra-long mat. Taller users should check they have enough room for roll-outs.
  • Natural rubber has a break-in smell and is not ideal for every household (pets, sensitivities, some floors). We have not used this mat, and the illustration is original artwork with no brand logo.

If your practice is on the floor, this is the serious home-mat option in this set of notes. If your practice is on a reformer, you want a carriage cover, not a 12 mm floor mat. If you are still deciding whether you will keep a home habit, it is reasonable to start with the mat you already own and revisit a $230 purchase once the routine is real.
