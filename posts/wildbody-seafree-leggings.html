Clothing

WildBody’s organic cotton leggings, made for actual work

$139 AUD

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) for the SeaFree Organic Cotton Leggings in Cacao: $139 AUD. Free standard shipping over $189 AUD.

Most pilates leggings are a nylon-and-elastane blend. WildBody’s SeaFree pair is a deliberate alternative: a 300 GSM fabric the brand calls SeaFree Cotton Flex, listed as 94% GOTS-certified organic cotton and 6% elastane, with OEKO-TEX dyes. The Cacao colour we looked at is $139 on the Australian site.

The listing leans on features people actually argue about in a class: a high waist, squat-proof coverage, no front seam, and in-built pockets. Sizes run from AU 4 to 22, with a published bust/waist/hip chart and a 69 cm inseam. The brand says the fit is true to size with gentle compression, and to size up if you are between sizes — the opposite of many compressive gym tights. It also notes that some sewn labels may still say 88/12, and that independent testing is what supports the 94/6 figure. That is an unusual admission, and worth knowing if you care about fibre claims.

Who it suits

People who want cotton against the skin, or who find polyester activewear unpleasant, and who will do pilates, strength work or general training rather than only lounging. The size range is also a genuine plus if you are outside the usual 6–14 window. It is less suitable if you want a buttery nylon feel (that is the Align end of the market), or if $139 feels like a lot before you have tried the fabric.

Pros

  • Organic cotton performance fabric, pockets, and a no-front-seam cut, on paper — the three things many people look for in a studio tight.
  • Custom-graded sizing from 4 to 22, with a clear chart and a stated “size up if between sizes” rule.
  • Australian fulfilment, plastic-free packaging, and free standard shipping over $189, which is one more piece away on a typical order.

Cons

  • $139 is a serious price for one pair. You should be confident about size, or ready to use the brand’s returns process, before you order.
  • Cotton-elastane fabrics can behave differently from nylon in heat and in very sweaty sessions. The brand positions these as workout tights, not loungewear, but we have not wear-tested them and cannot confirm squat-proofing or recovery in person.
  • The illustration on this page is AI-generated and is only an approximate likeness. It shows a front seam. The listing says there is no front seam. Trust the product page, not the picture.

Next to the lululemon Align crop, currently $89 on sale, SeaFree is dearer, cotton rather than Nulu, and sized for a wider range of bodies. If the fibre and the pockets matter more than a sale price, this is the more distinctive option. If you want the softest nylon crop for low-impact work and already know your lululemon size, the Align is the cheaper experiment while that sale lasts — with the caveat that Align sale styles may not accept change-of-mind returns.
