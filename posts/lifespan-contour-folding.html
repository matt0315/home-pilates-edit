Lifespan’s folding wooden reformer, currently $1,399

$1,399 AUD$2,399

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) for the Contour Folding Wooden Pilates Reformer Machine in beige (LFRF-CONTFLD2B): $1,399, with an RRP of $2,399. The page marked it in stock. This is a bulky item; check current delivery quotes for your address, especially in WA.

A home reformer is a piece of furniture as much as a piece of equipment. Lifespan Fitness’s Contour Folding wooden machine is the cheaper of the two folding reformers in this set of notes, and the one with the most aggressive sale price when we checked: $1,399, against an RRP of $2,399, for the beige maple-framed model, marked in stock.

The published specs are usefully specific. The machine is 241.5 by 66 by 63 cm in use, folds to 121 by 65.6 by 37 cm, sits on a 26 cm carriage height, and is rated to 110 kg with a maximum user height of 200 cm. There are five carbon-steel springs (three hard, one medium, one easy), a two-position spring platform, and the box, jump board and foot straps are included. The warranty on the page is 12 months for parts. Assembly is described as about 95% pre-assembled in a timber crate, which you then have to deal with.

Who it suits

Households that want a folding wooden reformer for an apartment or a spare room, at a mid price, and who can accept a 110 kg load rating and a one-year parts warranty. It is a weaker match if you are heavier than that rating, if you want a longer warranty, or if you would rather pay more for a brand with a dedicated pilates app and a longer track record in home reformers.

Pros

  • $1,399 is $1,000 below the listed RRP, and well below Your Reformer’s folding machine at $2,495.
  • Folded size is clearly stated, and the box and jump board are in the set, so you are not immediately buying extras.
  • Spring weights are published, which many prettier lifestyle brands do not bother to do.

Cons

  • A 12-month parts warranty is short for a machine this expensive. Your Reformer advertises a five-year limited warranty on its folding model. That gap is worth real thought.
  • The 110 kg load rating is lower than some people will need. Do not ignore it.
  • Delivery of an 86 kg crate is not a small-parcel problem, even if the page also talks about other delivery types. Get a quote for your address, plan for crate disposal, and put it on a flat, hard floor — Lifespan warns that thick carpet can make the carriage feel bumpy. We have not assembled or used this machine. The illustration is generic original artwork, not a brand photo.

This is the value folding reformer in the comparison: much cheaper than Your Reformer or Technogym, with honest published measurements, and with the obvious trade-offs of warranty, load rating and brand extras. If $1,399 is already a stretch, stay with classes and a ring for now. If you have the space and the budget, compare the warranty and stock of this machine with Your Reformer’s folding model before you pay a thousand dollars more — or less — for the name on the frame.
