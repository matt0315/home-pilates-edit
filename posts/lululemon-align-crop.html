lululemon Align No Line crop, on sale at $89

$89 AUD$129

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) for the Align No Line High-Rise Crop 21": $89, regularly $129. The page said the style is not eligible for change-of-mind returns, and that shipping is free with a lululemon account.

Align is lululemon’s soft, low-impact tight, the one people wear to yoga, pilates and a lot of ordinary days. The No Line High-Rise Crop 21" is the version without a front-rise seam, ending at the lower calf. On the Australian site, on the day we checked, it was $89, down from $129.

The fabric is Nulu: 81% nylon and 19% Lycra elastane, described as weightless, four-way stretch, sweat-wicking and breathable. There is a hidden waistband pocket for a card or a key. The page is clear that the collection is for low-impact workouts and for comfort, not for heavy gym sessions that can pill a delicate knit. A model note on the page puts a size 6 on a 175 cm frame, which is AU 8–10 in lululemon’s usual mapping, but you should still use the brand’s size guide rather than guessing from another label.

Who it suits

Anyone who already knows their Align size and wants a cropped, smooth-front tight for mat or reformer work, especially while the sale is on. It also suits people who prefer a buttery nylon hand-feel to cotton. It is a poorer fit for intense lifting, for anyone who is hard on seams and fabric, or for shoppers who might need a change-of-mind return on this particular style.

Pros

  • $89 is a real reduction from $129, on a fabric and cut a lot of people already know.
  • No front-rise seam, a high rise, and a tiny waistband pocket — useful on a reformer when you do not want a key in a side pocket under a strap.
  • Free shipping if you have a lululemon account, and you can check store stock if you would rather try it on.

Cons

  • The page says this style is not eligible for a change-of-mind return. That is a significant catch on a sale tight. Size carefully, or try it in store if you can.
  • Nulu is known for being delicate. If you sit on rough timber, velcro or an unpadded reformer spring, it can pill. That is a feature of the fabric, not a surprise defect.
  • We have not worn this crop. The illustration is original artwork, not a lululemon photograph, and it does not show a branded waistband or logo.

Compared with WildBody’s $139 cotton leggings, Align is on sale, nylon, and cropped. You are choosing a familiar low-impact fabric and a brand with stores, against a cotton tight with pockets and a wider published size range. If you already live in Align, $89 is a logical time to add a crop — provided you are sure of the size, because you may not be able to send it back for a change of mind.
