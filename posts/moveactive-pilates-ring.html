A $45 pilates ring from MoveActive

$45 AUD

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) for the Pilates Ring in black: $45 AUD. Listed as 37 cm (14.5 in) across, with a fibreglass inner ring, EVA foam and TPR handles. Free shipping over $65.

A pilates ring, or magic circle, is a simple tool: a flexible hoop with padded handles, used to add light resistance for inner thighs, arms and core work on the mat or, carefully, on a reformer. MoveActive’s black ring is $45, which is more than the ring inside a $30 Amazon kit, and less than you would spend getting a kit you only wanted one piece from.

The specification is clear. The ring is 37 cm (14.5 inches) across, with a fibreglass inner, EVA foam on the outside, and TPR handles. The brand tells you to use it for pilates and fitness work only, and to get instruction if you are not sure how to load it. On the day we checked there were 17 reviews on the official page, averaging 5.0, including comments from people using the rings in a studio and from travellers who liked that it packed in a suitcase.

Who it suits

People who already know they will use a ring, including teachers buying a spare for a home studio, and home practitioners who have been making do with a floppy kit ring. It is overkill if you only want to try three exercises once; in that case a budget kit is enough of a test.

Pros

  • A published construction (fibreglass, EVA, TPR) and diameter, which most bargain kits do not spell out this cleanly.
  • An Australian retailer with free shipping over $65 — add the medium band, or a pair of socks, and delivery is included.
  • Reviewers on the page mention grip and shape holding up better than rings that used to slip in their hands. That matches the usual reason people upgrade.

Cons

  • Seventeen reviews is not a huge sample, even at a high average. Read the most recent ones when you order.
  • $45 is more than a whole Amazon kit. You are buying one better-specified tool, not a bag of extras.
  • A ring is only as useful as the exercises you know. It will not replace a reformer, and used badly it can aggravate wrists or knees. We have not used this ring, and the illustration is original artwork.

If you are assembling a small, decent home kit from an Australian brand, this ring plus the $28 medium band is a cleaner start than a 20-piece mystery bag. If you are still deciding whether pilates at home will stick, buy the cheaper Amazon kit first and come back for a ring when the one in the bag starts to annoy you.
