Your Reformer’s folding machine, the $2,495 home option

$2,495 AUD$2,995

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) for the YR Original Folding: $2,495, regularly $2,995. The product page we opened also indicated the sale item may be sold out, so confirm stock before you plan around that price.

Your Reformer is the Australian home-reformer brand a lot of people already recognise from studios and social feeds. The YR Original Folding is the space-saving version of its main machine: the same idea as the non-folding Original, with a frame that folds away for apartments and shared rooms. On the day we checked, the listed price was $2,495, down from $2,995. The same page also indicated the sale item may be sold out, so the first job is to confirm that the colour you want is actually available.

What you are supposed to get for that money, according to the listing, is a maple timber frame, marine-grade upholstery in a choice of colours, a five-year limited warranty, and a bundle that includes a jump board, a box, a prop starter kit, and three months of the YR app. That last point matters: a machine without a plan is a very expensive clothes rack, and a short app window is a trial, not a lifetime of classes.

Who it suits

People who want a home machine that looks like furniture, who need it to fold, and who like the idea of a guided app to start with. It also suits buyers who care about a longer warranty than a typical fitness-retailer reformer. It is not a casual purchase, and it is not the right move if the sale size is already gone and you were only shopping the discounted figure.

Pros

  • A folding design aimed at real Australian apartments, with a finish that is meant to stay in a living room.
  • Five-year limited warranty, plus box, jump board and a starter prop kit, which would otherwise add up quickly.
  • Three months of app access is a proper onboarding window, even if you will eventually need another source of classes.

Cons

  • $2,495 is still a major outlay, and $500 off is only useful if the machine is in stock.
  • At almost twice the current Lifespan folding price, you should be able to say why: warranty, brand, app, finish and resale, not just the word “reformer”.
  • We have not used this machine, and we cannot verify current lead times, delivery to WA, or how the fold feels week to week. The illustration is original artwork, not a product photograph. If you are buying at this level, read the warranty wording and the current stock position on the page, and measure the in-use and folded footprint in your room.

If you have the budget and the brand is what you already know from class, this is the folding machine to shortlist. If you want the same category of product for less, Lifespan’s Contour Folding at $1,399 is the hard-nosed comparison — shorter warranty, lower load rating, less lifestyle polish. If money is not the constraint and you want a design-led machine with professional installation, Technogym Reform is in a different league again, at $18,550.
