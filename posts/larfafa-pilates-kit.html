LarFaFa’s pilates kit: more reviews, slower delivery

$38.35 AUD

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) for the LarFaFa Pilates Kit listing (ASIN B0FTFMN9QN): $38.35. A bestsellers list the same day still showed $35.99. The page said it is usually dispatched in 2–3 days, with delivery estimated 14–17 December, from a third-party seller rather than Amazon itself.

LarFaFa’s pilates kit is the other Amazon AU set that kept coming up in our shortlist: a ring-and-ball starter kit with bands, a strap, a bag and a 45-exercise guide, and a much larger review count than most of the kits around it. The listing we opened (ASIN B0FTFMN9QN) had 4.7 stars from 312 ratings, and a price of $38.35. A bestsellers list the same day still showed $35.99, so the live price is the one to trust.

That review volume is the main reason to look at it next to the cheaper Spopal set. The trade-off, at least on the day we checked, was fulfilment. The page said the item is usually dispatched in two to three days, with delivery estimated between 14 and 17 December, from a third-party seller, not from Amazon itself. If you are buying a kit for this week’s class, that timing is the story, not the extra couple of dollars.

Who it suits

Someone who wants a starter kit and prefers a listing with hundreds of reviews, and who can wait for a mid-December delivery. It is a poor choice if you need props immediately, or if you would rather deal with Amazon’s own dispatch when something is missing from the bag.

Pros

  • 312 reviews is a lot more signal than most of the pilates kits on the same bestsellers list.
  • The listed contents cover a proper starter spread: a fibreglass-reinforced ring, a small ball, several bands at different stated resistances, a stretching strap and a guide.
  • Still firmly in “try this at home” money, even at $38.35.

Cons

  • Third-party fulfilment and a mid-December delivery window when we checked. This is not a next-day spare for tomorrow’s class.
  • The product title on Amazon also wanders through other names, including “Better Me”. That kind of messy titling is common on this end of the marketplace and is a reminder to read the current seller and dispatch details, not just the brand name.
  • As with any budget kit, resistance numbers and ring stiffness can vary. We have not used this set, and the illustration is generic artwork, not a pack shot.

Choose LarFaFa if the review count matters and you can wait. Choose Spopal if you want the cheaper, Amazon-fulfilled set that was in stock for earlier delivery. If you already know the ring is the piece you will use, skip the kit and buy a single MoveActive ring instead.
