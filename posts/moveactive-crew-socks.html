Grip socks

MoveActive’s black crew grip socks, the studio staple

$22.95 AUD

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) for Crew Grip Socks in black. Free standard shipping on orders over $65. Delivery to WA was listed as 7–10 business days standard, 3–5 days express.

MoveActive is one of the Australian brands that actually specialises in grip socks for pilates, barre and yoga, rather than treating them as a side product. The black crew pair is the plain, studio-staple version: $22.95 on the day we checked, with 859 reviews and an average just under 4.9 out of 5 on the brand’s site.

Unlike one-size Amazon packs, these are sold in sizes from XS to XL, with a chart that maps to US, UK and EU shoe sizes. The composition is listed as 75% cotton, 17% polyester and 8% elastane, with PVC grips on the sole, an elastic cuff and arch compression. The brand recommends sizing down if you are between sizes. Free shipping kicks in over $65, which is two or three pairs, or a pair plus a prop.

Who it suits

Anyone who is in a regular reformer or barre class and wants a sock that is sized, widely reviewed, and easy to reorder in the same colour. It also suits people who have had one-size socks slip at the heel. It is not the cheapest option, and it is not the one to pick if you only need a spare pair for a single trial class.

Pros

  • Proper sizing, including a note to size down when you are in between, which is more useful than “one size fits most”.
  • A large review base on the official site, and a style that studios actually stock.
  • Crew length covers the ankle on a chilly studio floor, and the black colour goes with everything.
  • Free shipping over $65, with express as an option if you are not in WA; Perth and the Territory should plan for the longer standard window of 7–10 business days.

Cons

  • $22.95 is a lot next to a $3-ish Amazon pair. You are paying for fit, brand and (hopefully) durability, not for a bargain pack.
  • A minority of reviews on the page mention socks that look good but feel less grippy or less secure than other MoveActive styles they already own. That is worth reading in the current reviews, because quality can vary by style and batch.
  • We have not worn this pair. The illustration is original artwork, not a product photograph.

If you already like MoveActive, the black crew is the obvious repeat buy. If you are choosing a first “good” pair, this is the one we would shortlist next to All Fenix: more sizes and more reviews here, a lower sale price there. For a throwaway first class, the ZANZER pack is cheaper. For a sock you will wear twice a week, paying for a size chart is usually the better spend.
