Mats

A washable mat for the reformer carriage, not the floor

$79 AUD

Price as seen on 25 November 2026 (Australia/Perth) for the Microfibre Reformer Mat in Ivory: $79 AUD. Listed size is 94 × 56 cm, plus a 27 × 19 cm neck section, at 2 mm thick. Free shipping over $65.

This is not a floor yoga mat. MoveActive’s microfibre reformer mat is a thin cover that sits on the carriage of a reformer, with a narrower section for the headrest. On the day we checked, the ivory version was $79. The listed size is 94 by 56 cm for the body, plus 27 by 19 cm for the neck, at 2 mm thick and about 500 g, with a carry pouch.

The fabric is a recycled-polyester microfibre suede on top, with a rubber underside (the listing breaks that out as a mix of natural rubber and rubber). It is described as machine-washable in cold water, and as fitting most reformers. That last claim is the one to be careful with: “most” is not the same as “yours”. If you already have a machine, check the carriage measurements against 94 by 56 cm before you buy, especially on compact or folding frames.

Who it suits

People who already go to a reformer class, or who own a home machine, and want a washable, grippy layer for hygiene and a bit of cushion, plus something that covers the headrest. It does nothing for you if you only practise on the floor — that is a different mat, such as Manduka’s Cloud.

Pros

  • A carriage-specific shape, including the neck, which a folded towel does not do as neatly.
  • Machine-washable, with a carry pouch, which is the practical reason studio clients buy these.
  • A large review base on the official page (174 reviews, averaging about 4.95 when we looked), including studio owners talking about wipe-down and fit.

Cons

  • $79 is a lot for a 2 mm cover. You are paying for fit, fabric and washability, not for cushioning like a 12 mm floor mat.
  • Compatibility is “most reformers”, not a guaranteed match. Measure first.
  • The rubber backing and flammability warning are worth reading in the care notes. We have not used this mat, and the illustration is original artwork, not a brand photo.

If you are building a home studio, this is a comfort and hygiene extra, not a substitute for a floor mat or for the machine itself. If you do not yet own a reformer, spend the money on classes or on a ring and a band instead, and come back to a carriage mat when you have a carriage to put it on.
