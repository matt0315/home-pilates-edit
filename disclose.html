Affiliate and AI disclosure

Affiliate links

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. That means The Home Pilates Edit may earn a commission if you click through and buy, at no extra cost to you. It does not change the price you pay.

We expect to use, once accepted: Amazon Associates AU, Commission Factory, Awin and Impact, plus any direct programs a brand already runs in Australia. Until an application is approved and a tracking ID is added, links on this site are ordinary, untagged product URLs.

We do not accept payment from a brand for a favourable write-up, and we do not pretend to have used a product we have only seen on a listing. When we have not worn, used or independently tested an item, the note says so.

AI-generated imagery and video

Some images on this site are AI-generated illustrations. Each of those images is labelled. They are created to show a type of product in a simple setting. They are not photographs of the exact retail item, not photos of a real customer, and not reviews.

If we later share short videos on TikTok that use AI-generated imagery, those clips should also be labelled with TikTok’s AI-generated content setting, and should not be presented as real customer reviews.

AI illustrations can get details wrong. On the WildBody SeaFree note, for example, the illustration shows a front seam; the retailer’s listing describes a no-front-seam design. Always treat the product page as the source of truth for colour, cut and features.

Prices and availability

Prices are in Australian dollars and dated, with the timezone Australia/Perth. Stock, delivery dates, sale periods and return rules change. Confirm everything on the retailer’s page before you buy, especially on higher-priced reformers and on sale items marked as final sale.

How we keep links in one place

Every product URL lives in a single file, data/links.json. When a tracking tag or network deep link is added there, the buttons on the site pick it up automatically. You do not need to edit each post.
